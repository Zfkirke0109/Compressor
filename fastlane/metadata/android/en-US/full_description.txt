<b>Galaxy Compressor</b> compresses batches of videos on your Android device using Media3 and the phone’s hardware encoders. Select videos in the app or share them from your gallery.

<b>Perceptually Lossless</b> searches for a smaller output and checks its timeline, audio and color information against the source. The app compares decoded frames in sampled windows and keeps the original when quality, size or verification requirements are unmet. Exhaustive mode can try a safer bitrate after an unsuccessful attempt. The Galaxy S26 Ultra and S23 Ultra use separate learned encoder profiles.

<b>Copy and recovery</b> let you keep originals while reviewing results. If you enable replacement, the app stages recovery data and verifies gallery backups and copies by reading them back. Each batch row reports what happened to its original and output.

<b>Diagnostics</b> include per-file results and a log-only ZIP you can export to Downloads. Video processing happens on the device. Compatible tracks can also be remuxed without re-encoding.

The GitHub release APK supports 64-bit ARM devices running Android 7.0 or later.
